FINDING YOUR EVERYDAY MEAL SHOULDN'T BE THIS HARD.

For many students and working professionals, finding a reliable everyday meal option can still involve unnecessary searching, uncertainty and manual processes.

📍

Finding the right mess

Finding a suitable mess around you can take time, especially when you don't know which options are actually available nearby.

🍱

Not knowing what's available

It can be difficult to know which messes offer meals, what plans they have, and whether they're suitable for your daily routine.

💬

Too much manual coordination

Calling or messaging mess owners to ask about plans, availability, timings, or meal access can become repetitive and inconvenient.

📅

Managing your daily meals

Keeping track of your subscription and remembering where, when, and how you can use your meal access isn't always simple.

🎟️

No simple way to reserve

Traditional mess systems often don't provide an easy digital way to reserve your meal and confirm your access before visiting.

📱

Everything is scattered

Finding a mess, choosing a plan, managing meal access, making reservations, and keeping track of everything can happen through different channels instead of one simple platform.

ONE SUBSCRIPTION. YOUR TOKENS. YOUR CHOICE.

MessClub uses tokens to make meal access simple and easy to understand. One token is one meal — a lunch and a dinner every day.

  • Your plan, your pace. Tokens don't vanish on a calendar date — you keep them until you've used them.
  • Plans for every budget. Silver, Golden and Premium give you wider and wider choice of messes.
  • New here? A one-day first-time trial lets you try 2 tokens at any approved mess.
